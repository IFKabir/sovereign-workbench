# API 520: Sizing, Selection, and Installation of Pressure-Relieving Devices

## 1. Scope
This standard covers the sizing and selection of pressure relief devices used in refineries and related industries for equipment that has a maximum allowable working pressure (MAWP) of 15 psig or greater.

## 2. Relief Valve Sizing Methodology
Relief valves are sized based on the worst-case overpressure scenario. The required relief area is calculated to ensure the accumulated pressure does not exceed allowable limits.

## 3. Overpressure Scenarios and Contingencies
Common scenarios include:
- External Fire (pool fire, jet fire)
- Blocked discharge
- Control valve failure (wide open)
- Thermal expansion
- Power failure / Cooling water failure

## 4. Calculation Procedures
- **Gas/Vapor Relief**: Based on critical or sub-critical flow equations.
- **Steam Relief**: Based on Napier's equation.
- **Liquid Relief**: Based on Bernoulli's principle. Area = Flow / (Kd * Kv * sqrt(2 * deltaP / density)).

## 5. Set Pressure Determination
The set pressure of a PRV shall typically not exceed the MAWP of the protected equipment. For fire sizing, the set pressure can be up to 105% MAWP, with maximum accumulation of 121%.
