# OISD Standard 118: Layouts for Oil and Gas Installations (Excerpt)

## 1. Introduction
This standard lays down the minimum requirements for layout of various facilities in oil and gas installations including refineries, such as Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals Limited (MRPL). The proper layout of facilities is essential to minimize the consequences of accidents and to prevent fire or explosion from spreading.

## 2. Separation Distances
The safety distances are essential to prevent the spread of fire. The safe separation distances between process units, tanks, and buildings must be maintained as follows:

| From / To | Process Unit | Storage Tank | Control Room | Fire Station | Property Line |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Process Unit** | 30m | 60m | 60m | 60m | 50m |
| **Storage Tank** | 60m | 0.5D (Min 15m) | 60m | 60m | 50m |
| **Control Room** | 60m | 60m | - | 30m | 30m |
| **Fire Station** | 60m | 60m | 30m | - | 30m |
*Note: Distances are in meters. D = Diameter of the larger tank.*

## 3. Road Widths and Access
All units shall be accessible from at least two sides. Minimum road width around a process unit shall be 12 meters to allow movement of fire tenders. Fire station placement should be such that fire tenders can reach the incident spot within 3 minutes of receiving a call.

## 4. Specific Measurements
- Distance between fired heaters and other process equipment: Min 15m.
- Separation between flare stack and property line: Min 90m (or as calculated by dispersion models).
- Boundary wall height shall be a minimum of 3 meters.

## 5. References to Codes
This document references IS codes (e.g., IS 3016, IS 3218) and API Standards (API 650 for tanks, API 520/521 for relief systems).

For a coastal installation like MRPL, prevailing wind direction throughout the year must be considered.
