# OISD-STD-105: Work Permit System

## 1. Objective
To establish a safe procedure for carrying out various types of works in hazardous areas like MRPL process units.

## 2. Types of Permits
1. **Hot Work Permit**: For jobs that involve or may generate a source of ignition (welding, cutting). Color: Pink.
2. **Cold Work Permit**: For routine maintenance not involving hot work. Color: Yellow.
3. **Confined Space Entry**: For entry into vessels, tanks, trenches deeper than 1.2m. Color: Blue.
4. **Excavation Permit**: For digging or excavation exceeding 0.3m. Color: Green.

## 3. PTW Issuance Procedure
- Initiator requests permit.
- Area Operator prepares equipment (depressurizes, flushes, isolates).
- Area Shift-in-Charge checks isolation.
- Safety Officer approval requirements: All Hot Work in operating units and Confined Space Entries require Safety Officer's counter-signature.
- Issuer signs, Executant signs.

## 4. Isolation Protocols (Double Block and Bleed)
For positive isolation before hot work on hydrocarbon lines, a Double Block and Bleed (DBB) arrangement must be verified. The bleed valve must be open to atmosphere to confirm zero pressure between the two blocks. If DBB is not available, blinding/spading is mandatory.

## 5. PPE Requirements
- **Cold Work**: Hard hat, safety shoes, coveralls, cotton gloves.
- **Hot Work**: Welding shield, leather apron, leather gloves, FR clothing.
- **Confined Space**: Full body harness, SCBA/Airline mask, multi-gas detector.
